## Description (App Store / Google Play)

Wake up naturally and effortlessly with SleepWise — a smart alarm clock that analyzes your sleep and wakes you at the perfect moment.

━━━ HOW IT WORKS ━━━

SleepWise uses your smartphone's microphone to analyze sounds during sleep. The app identifies sleep phases — light sleep, deep sleep, and REM (Rapid Eye Movement) — by detecting characteristic patterns of breathing, movement, and ambient sounds.

You set a wake-up time and a window of 10–30 minutes. The alarm triggers during a light sleep phase within that window, when your body is most ready to wake up. The result — you rise feeling alert and rested, without that groggy feeling.

━━━ KEY FEATURES ━━━

◆ SMART ALARM
Wakes you during the optimal sleep phase within your chosen wake-up window. Gradual volume increase provides a gentle, natural awakening.

◆ NIGHT MODE
An elegant dark clock display during tracking. Minimal brightness won't disturb your sleep, yet keeps the time visible.

◆ SLEEP ANALYSIS
A detailed hypnogram shows all your sleep phases: falling asleep, light sleep, deep sleep, and REM. See exactly how much time you spend in each phase.

◆ MORNING REPORT
Every morning — a personalized sleep quality score (0–100) with detailed metrics: total duration, time to fall asleep, number of awakenings, deep sleep percentage.

◆ SNORE DETECTION
The app captures snoring episodes, their duration, and intensity. Track trends and take steps to improve your sleep quality.

◆ STATISTICS & TRENDS
Charts for week, month, and year: sleep quality dynamics, average bedtime and wake time, sleep duration. Track progress and discover patterns.

◆ HEALTH INTEGRATION
Sync data with Apple Health and Google Fit. All sleep metrics accessible within your unified health ecosystem.

◆ SOUND LIBRARY
Choose from a collection of pleasant alarm sounds: from soft melodies to nature sounds. Customize volume and fade-in duration.

━━━ PRIVACY & SECURITY ━━━

Your privacy is our top priority:

• Audio is NEVER recorded or stored. Analysis happens in real time on-device — sound is processed and immediately discarded.
• Sleep data is stored only on your device.
• No data is sent to any servers. All processing is local.
• The app requires no registration and collects no personal information.
• Microphone access is used exclusively for sleep phase analysis.
