Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

SleepWise ("the App", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Sleep Metrics (Stored Locally)

  • Sleep duration, bedtime, and wake time
  • Sleep phases (light, deep, REM, awake)
  • Sleep quality score
  • Snoring episodes (duration and count)
  • Number of nighttime awakenings

1.2 Device Permissions

  • Microphone: Used exclusively for real-time audio analysis to determine sleep phases. Audio is processed on-device and never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Notifications: Used to trigger alarm sounds and sleep reminders.
  • Health (Apple Health / Google Fit): Optional. Used to read heart rate data and write sleep session data. Only accessed with your explicit permission.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Audio recordings: We do NOT record, store, or transmit any audio. Sound is analyzed in real time and immediately discarded.
  • Personal information: No names, emails, phone numbers, or PII.
  • Location data: We do not access your location.
  • Analytics: No third-party analytics SDKs in the free version.
  • Advertising data: No ads, no data shared with advertisers.

3. Data Storage

All sleep data is stored exclusively on your device using local encrypted storage. We do not operate servers that receive or store your data.

If you enable Apple Health or Google Fit integration, sleep data is written to those platforms according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to any third parties.

5. Data Retention

Sleep data remains on your device until you delete it within the App, clear App data through device settings, or uninstall the App. We do not retain any data after uninstallation.

6. Children's Privacy

SleepWise does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App requires no registration and collects no personal information.

7. Security

We employ local encryption for all stored sleep data. Since no data leaves your device, the risk of data breach is minimal.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: View all your sleep data within the App
  • Delete: Remove any or all sleep sessions at any time
  • Portability: Export your data (Pro feature)
  • Revoke permissions: Disable microphone or Health access in device settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, please contact us:

  • Email: support@sleepwise.app
  • GitHub: github.com/sleepwise/sleepwise/issues

This Privacy Policy applies to the SleepWise application on the App Store, Google Play, and RuStore.
