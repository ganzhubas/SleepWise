# Privacy Policy — SleepWise

**Last updated:** February 19, 2026

SleepWise ("the App", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## 1. Data We Collect

### 1.1 Sleep Metrics (Collected Locally)
- Sleep duration, bedtime, and wake time
- Sleep phases (light, deep, REM, awake)
- Sleep quality score
- Snoring episodes (duration and count)
- Number of nighttime awakenings

### 1.2 Device Permissions
- **Microphone**: Used exclusively for real-time audio analysis to determine sleep phases. Audio is processed on-device and **never recorded, stored, or transmitted**.
- **Notifications**: Used to trigger alarm sounds and sleep reminders.
- **Health (Apple Health / Google Fit)**: Optional. Used to read heart rate data (if available) and write sleep session data. Only accessed with your explicit permission.

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

- **Audio recordings**: We do NOT record, store, or transmit any audio. Sound is analyzed in real time and immediately discarded.
- **Personal information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.
- **Location data**: We do not access or store your location.
- **Usage analytics**: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs in the free version.
- **Advertising data**: We do not serve ads or share data with advertisers.

## 3. Data Storage

All sleep data is stored **exclusively on your device** using local encrypted storage. We do not operate servers that receive or store your data.

If you enable Apple Health or Google Fit integration, sleep data is written to those platforms according to their respective privacy policies. You can revoke this access at any time through your device settings.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to any third parties.

The only data exchange occurs when you explicitly enable Health integration, in which case data is shared with Apple Health or Google Fit under your control.

## 5. Data Retention

Sleep data remains on your device until you:
- Delete individual sleep sessions within the App
- Clear the App's data through device settings
- Uninstall the App

We do not retain any data after the App is uninstalled.

## 6. Children's Privacy

SleepWise does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). The App does not require registration and does not collect personal information from any user.

## 7. Security

We employ local encryption for all stored sleep data. Since no data leaves your device (unless you opt into Health integration), the risk of data breach through our App is minimal.

## 8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:
- **Access**: View all your sleep data within the App
- **Delete**: Remove any or all sleep sessions at any time
- **Portability**: Export your data through the App's export feature (Pro)
- **Revoke permissions**: Disable microphone or Health access in device settings at any time

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
